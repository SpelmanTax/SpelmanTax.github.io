Accountant for Surveyors (Geometri) – Bolzano and all of Italy

A surveyor's practice requires precision, organisation and correct tax management. I offer a dedicated service for geometri in Bolzano and South Tyrol and for technical professionals throughout Italy, covering invoicing, tax regime, Cassa Geometri, tax returns and annual filings.

Goal: clear, orderly and error-free tax management.

Invoices for professional services

Surveyors invoice technical services, consulting, appraisals, surveys and land registry work. I make sure invoices are handled correctly and meet all tax requirements.

  • Invoices for technical services
  • Withholding tax (where applicable)
  • Recharged expenses
  • Digital storage of documents

Tax regime for surveyors

I help you choose the most suitable tax regime: flat-rate (forfettario) or ordinary. The choice depends on turnover, costs and how your practice is structured.

Flat-rate regime: ideal for those with low costs who want simple management.

Pension contributions – Cassa Geometri or INPS separate scheme

Surveyors registered with the professional register have their own pension fund (Cassa Geometri). Those who are not registered contribute to the INPS separate scheme (Gestione Separata).

  • Registration with Cassa Geometri
  • Calculation of pension contributions
  • INPS separate scheme (if not registered)
