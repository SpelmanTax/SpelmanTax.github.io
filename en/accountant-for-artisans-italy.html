Accountant for Artisans – Bolzano and all of Italy

A craft business needs precise, orderly and compliant tax management. I offer a dedicated service for artisans in Bolzano and South Tyrol and for craftsmen throughout Italy, covering invoicing, tax regime, INPS contributions, tax returns and annual filings.

Goal: clear, simple and error-free tax management.

Invoices for craft work

Artisans invoice manual work, maintenance, repairs, installations and technical services. I make sure invoices are handled correctly and meet all tax requirements.

  • Invoices for craft work
  • Withholding tax (where applicable)
  • Recharged expenses
  • Digital storage of documents

Tax regime for artisans

I help you choose the most suitable tax regime: flat-rate (forfettario) or ordinary. The choice depends on turnover, costs and how your business is structured.

Flat-rate regime: ideal for those with low costs who want simple management.

INPS – Artisans and Traders scheme

Artisans are registered with the INPS Artigiani scheme, with fixed contributions and contributions based on income.

  • Registration with INPS Artigiani
  • Calculation of fixed and variable contributions
  • F24 payments for contributions

Annual obligations

  • Income tax return
  • VAT settlements and returns (under the ordinary regime)
  • F24 payments
  • Monitoring of deadlines and obligations

Administrative support

  • Employment contracts

  • Documentation for building work

Personal assistance
