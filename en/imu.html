IMU – Municipal Property Tax (Italy)

I provide a complete IMU service for property owners in Italy and abroad. This includes the calculation of advance and balance payments, verification of cadastral data, and preparation of F24 payment forms.

Goal: ensuring accurate, timely and fully compliant IMU calculations.

IMU Calculation: Advance and Balance

IMU is calculated based on the revalued cadastral value, municipal tax rates, and any reductions or exemptions provided by the Municipality of Bolzano.

  • Advance payment (June)
  • Balance payment (December)
  • Verification of updated municipal rates
  • Check of property categories and appurtenances

Cadastral Data Verification

Before calculating IMU, I verify all cadastral data to avoid errors and inconsistencies.

  • Cadastral value
  • Category and class
  • Ownership percentage
  • Months of ownership
  • Possible reductions (e.g., family use, appurtenances)

Correct cadastral verification prevents mistakes in calculation and payments.

Preparation of F24 Forms

I prepare F24 forms for IMU payments with correct tax codes and precise amounts.

  • F24 advance payment
  • F24 balance payment
  • Updated tax codes
  • Telematic submission or PDF delivery

IMU for Non-Residents

  • IMU calculation for second homes
  • Payments from abroad
  • Support available in English, German and Russian
