About me

I am Galja Spelman, Dottore Commercialista, Certified Public Accountant (CPA) and Steuerberaterin. I provide tax and accounting consultancy for freelancers, sole traders and businesses in Italy, with a particular focus on cross‑border situations and international taxation.

I help freelancers, consultants and small business owners manage their VAT position, taxes and tax returns in a simple, clear and solution‑oriented way.

My services include:

Opening a Partita IVA
Flat‑rate tax regime (Regime forfettario)
Tax consultancy for freelancers and businesses
International taxation and cross‑border tax matters

I also offer online consultancy in Italian, German, English and Russian. If you need tax support or want to open a Partita IVA, feel free to contact me for a consultation.
