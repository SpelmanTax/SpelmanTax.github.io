Why doctors choose an accountant who knows the healthcare sector

Running a Partita IVA as a doctor means more than bookkeeping: healthcare invoices, INPS contributions, deductions, tax regimes and reporting to the Sistema Tessera Sanitaria.

That is why many doctors prefer an accountant who understands the needs of healthcare professionals and offers clear, fast and personal assistance.

Typical issues for doctors with a VAT number (and how I handle them)

  • Healthcare invoices: intramoenia, extramoenia, withholding tax, formal errors.
  • Choice of tax regime: flat-rate (forfettario), ordinary, changes of regime.
  • Deductible professional expenses: CME training, insurance, car, practice.
  • INPS contributions: separate scheme, minimum amounts, rates.
  • Ongoing administrative support: deadlines, F24 payments, documentation.
  • Healthcare expense reporting (STS): mandatory electronic transmission.

Reporting patients' healthcare expenses to the Italian Revenue Agency

Sending healthcare expenses to the Sistema Tessera Sanitaria (STS) is mandatory for all doctors who issue healthcare invoices.

I take care of:

  • Checking healthcare invoices.
  • Checking patient data.
  • Electronic STS transmission.
  • Managing receipts and notices.
